Faith Under the Stars ("we," "us") runs faithunderthestars.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

What we collect

  • Email signups. When you request the free guide, we collect your first name and email address.
  • Purchases. When you buy the journal, our checkout provider ([Stripe / Lemon Squeezy / Gumroad]) collects your name, email, and payment details. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Analytics and advertising. If you accept cookies, we use Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel to understand which pages and posts bring visitors here and to measure our ads. These tools may collect your IP address, device and browser information, and pages visited.

How we use it

  • To send the guide you asked for, and follow-up emails about faith, the sky, and our products. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To deliver your purchase and send your receipt.
  • To understand what's helpful and improve the site and our Instagram content.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run this site: our email service ([Kit / MailerLite]), our checkout provider, our website host ([Netlify / Vercel / Cloudflare]), Google Analytics, and Meta. They process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies.

Cookies and your choices

Analytics and advertising cookies load only after you click Accept on the cookie banner. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser and reload the page. You can also opt out of personalized ads in your Google and Meta account settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. To access, correct, or delete your information, email hello@faithunderthestars.com. Depending on where you live (for example, California or the EU/UK), you may have additional rights, and we'll honor them.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll change the date at the top of this page.
